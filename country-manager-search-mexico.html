What Makes a Country Manager Search Different

A country manager search is not a standard executive search. The candidate needs to satisfy two audiences simultaneously: headquarters, which needs someone who reports clearly and operates within the global structure, and the local team, which needs someone who understands Mexico — its business culture, its labor law, its relationship dynamics.

Most global search firms search their own database and present whoever is available. We start by mapping the market: who currently holds a comparable role, who is performing well, and who would consider a move for the right opportunity. Then we approach them confidentially.

Our Search Process

1
Brief and market mapping
We spend the first week understanding the role: what the business needs, what the Mexico operation looks like, what success means in year one, and what kind of leader fits both headquarters and the local team. Then we map who exists in the market.
2
Confidential outreach
We approach the strongest candidates directly and confidentially. We do not post the role publicly unless the client asks. The best country manager candidates are not browsing job boards.
3
Interviews and assessment
We interview every candidate ourselves before presenting them. We assess bilingual communication, leadership style, cultural fit, and track record. We share our assessment alongside the candidate profile.
4
Shortlist presentation
You receive 3 to 5 candidates with full profiles, our written assessment, compensation benchmarks, and a recommendation on who we would start with and why.
5
Offer and onboarding support
We manage the offer process, including compensation negotiation, and stay involved through the first 90 days to make sure the transition is solid.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a country manager search take?
Most searches deliver a shortlist in 4 to 6 weeks. The full process — from brief to accepted offer — typically takes 8 to 12 weeks. Speed depends on how clearly the role is defined and how quickly your team can interview.
What does a country manager earn in Mexico?
Total compensation for a country manager in Mexico typically ranges from MXN $3.5M to $7M annually, depending on industry, company size, and whether the role includes equity or expat benefits. We provide a full compensation benchmark at the start of every search.
Do you work on retainer or contingency?
We work on retainer for country manager and C-suite searches. This means we commit dedicated resources to your search and you receive our full attention — not a speculative effort that depends on a placement fee.
Can you search for a plant manager or VP as well as a country manager?
Yes. We place country managers, directors general, general managers, plant managers, VPs, and other senior leaders reporting to them. If you are building a leadership team for a new Mexico operation, we can support multiple searches.
What if the hire doesn't work out?
We offer a 12-month guarantee on all retained searches. If the placed candidate leaves or is let go within the first year for performance reasons, we conduct a replacement search at no additional fee.
